# MCC NEET UG Counselling Rules, Guidelines and Policy FAQ (2024–2025)

## 1. Overview of Counselling Quotas
Medical Counselling Committee (MCC), Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) conducts online counselling for:
1. **15% All India Quota (AIQ)** seats in all Government Medical and Dental colleges across India (including Jammu & Kashmir).
2. **100% MBBS/BDS seats** in All India Institutes of Medical Sciences (AIIMS) and JIPMER (Puducherry & Karaikal).
3. **100% MBBS/BDS seats** in Central Universities (AMU, BHU, Jamia Millia Islamia) and Institutional Quota in Delhi University (MAMC, LHMC, UCMS).
4. **100% seats** in Deemed Universities.
5. **State Quota (85%)**: Conducted separately by respective State Counselling Authorities (e.g., UPDGME for UP, KEA for Karnataka, WBMCC for West Bengal, Maharashtra CET Cell).

## 2. Round-by-Round Rules and Seat Upgradation

### Rule 2.1: Round 1 (Free Exit)
- If a candidate is allotted a seat in Round 1 and does not wish to join, they can exercise **Free Exit** without forfeiture of their refundable security deposit.
- If a candidate joins the Round 1 allotted college, they can give willingness for **Upgradation in Round 2**.

### Rule 2.2: Round 2 (Exit with Forfeiture)
- Candidates can register afresh in Round 2 if not registered in Round 1.
- If a candidate is allotted a new seat or upgraded seat in Round 2, they must report to the college.
- **Exit with Forfeiture**: If a seat is allotted in Round 2 and the candidate does not join, their refundable security deposit is **forfeited**.
- If a candidate joins the Round 2 allotted seat, they **cannot resign or surrender** the seat and become ineligible for subsequent state or AIQ rounds.

### Rule 2.3: Round 3 (Mop-Up Round) & Stray Vacancy
- Fresh registration is allowed in Round 3 for unregistered candidates.
- Once a candidate is admitted in Round 3, they cannot participate in Stray Vacancy rounds.
- Stray Vacancy round is conducted strictly without new registration; un-allotted registered candidates are considered.
- If a candidate is allotted a seat in Stray Vacancy and does not join, they are **debarred from appearing in the NEET UG exam for the subsequent one year**, and their security deposit is forfeited.

## 3. Security Deposit & Registration Fees

### Government / Central Universities / AIQ:
- Non-refundable Registration Fee: ₹1,000 (General/EWS/OBC) or ₹500 (SC/ST/PwD).
- Refundable Security Deposit: **₹10,000** (General/EWS) or **₹5,000** (Reserved categories).

### Deemed Universities:
- Non-refundable Registration Fee: ₹5,000 (all candidates).
- Refundable Security Deposit: **₹2,00,000** (all candidates).

## 4. Mandatory Documents Required at Reporting
During physical reporting to the allotted college, original copies with 2 sets of attested photocopies are mandatory:
1. NEET UG Admit Card issued by NTA.
2. NEET UG Score Card / Rank Letter.
3. Class 10th Certificate and Mark Sheet (for Date of Birth verification).
4. Class 12th Certificate and Mark Sheet.
5. Eight (8) passport-size photographs (same as affixed on the application form).
6. Provisional Allotment Letter generated online from MCC portal.
7. Proof of Identity (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, PAN Card, or Driving License).
8. Relevant Caste / Category Certificate (OBC-NCL / EWS / SC / ST / PwD) issued by competent authority in the prescribed format.

## 5. Platform Ethical Policy & Counselling Disclaimers (NEET.info)
- **No Admission Guarantee**: NEET.info algorithms and counsellors provide predictive analysis based strictly on historical cut-off ranks, category seat matrices, and statistical trends. NEET.info **does not guarantee** admission into any college or course.
- **Independence & Transparency**: NEET.info operates independently and does **not accept commissions or incentives** from any private or deemed medical colleges. Revenue is derived solely from student subscriptions.
- **Official Verification**: Final cut-offs and seat allocations are decided solely by the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) and State Authorities. Candidates must verify all cut-offs against official MCC publications before making binding choices.
